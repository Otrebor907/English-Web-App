### Obiettivo della lezione

Alla fine della lezione saprai usare *was* e *were* per descrivere persone, luoghi, stati e situazioni nel passato.

### Regola e uso

- Il past simple di *to be* ha due forme: **was** con *I, he, she, it* e **were** con *you, we, they*.
- La negativa è **wasn't / weren't**.
- La domanda si forma invertendo verbo e soggetto: **Was she...? Were they...?**
- Le risposte brevi sono *Yes, I was / No, I wasn't* e *Yes, they were / No, they weren't*.
- Espressioni frequenti: *yesterday, last night, last week, two years ago, in 2020*.

### Struttura

| Funzione | Forma | Esempio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affermativa singolare | I/he/she/it + was | She was tired. |
| Affermativa plurale | you/we/they + were | They were late. |
| Negativa | wasn't / weren't | We weren't at home. |
| Domanda | Was/Were + soggetto? | Were you busy? |

### Esempi con traduzione

- **I was at work yesterday.** — Ieri ero al lavoro.
- **She was very tired last night.** — Ieri sera era molto stanca.
- **We were in Paris in May.** — Eravamo a Parigi a maggio.
- **The shops were closed.** — I negozi erano chiusi.
- **He wasn't at the meeting.** — Non era alla riunione.
- **They weren't ready.** — Non erano pronti.
- **Was the film good?** — Il film era bello?
- **Were you at home?** — Eri a casa?

### Errori tipici per chi parla italiano

- ❌ **I were tired.** → ✅ **I was tired.** — Con *I* si usa *was*.
- ❌ **You was late.** → ✅ **You were late.** — Con *you* si usa *were*.
- ❌ **Did she was at home?** → ✅ **Was she at home?** — Con *to be* non si usa *did*.
- ❌ **We didn't be ready.** → ✅ **We weren't ready.** — La negativa di *to be* usa *wasn't/weren't*.

### Esercizio guidato

1. Completa: I ___ at school yesterday. **Risposta: was.**
2. Completa: They ___ happy. **Risposta: were.**
3. Rendi negativa: He was here. **Risposta: He wasn't here.**
4. Fai la domanda: You were tired. **Risposta: Were you tired?**

### Esercizio finale

1. Completa: Anna ___ in London last week.
2. Completa: We ___ very busy yesterday.
3. Completa: The weather ___ cold.
4. Rendi negativa: They were at the party.
5. Trasforma in domanda: Marco was ill.
6. Completa: ___ you at work on Monday?
7. Completa la risposta: Was she late? No, ___.
8. Correggi: *I were at home.*
9. Correggi: *Did they were happy?*
10. Traduci: «Due anni fa eravamo studenti.»

### Soluzioni e spiegazioni

1. **was**. Anna equivale a *she*.
2. **were**. Con *we* si usa *were*.
3. **was**. *The weather* equivale a *it*.
4. **They weren't at the party.**
5. **Was Marco ill?**
6. **Were**.
7. **No, she wasn't.**
8. **I was at home.**
9. **Were they happy?**
10. **We were students two years ago.**

### Riepilogo

- *Was* con I/he/she/it; *were* con you/we/they.
- Negativa: *wasn't/weren't*.
- Domanda: inverti *was/were* e soggetto.
- Non usare *did* con il passato di *to be*.
