### Obiettivo della lezione

Alla fine della lezione saprai usare il past continuous per descrivere un'azione in corso nel passato e combinarlo con il past simple.

### Regola e uso

- Il past continuous si forma con **was/were + verbo in -ing**: *I was working; they were working*.
- Usalo per un'azione in corso in un momento passato: *At eight, I was having dinner*.
- Usalo come sfondo di una storia; il past simple indica spesso l'evento breve che interrompe: *I was walking when it started to rain*.
- Con due azioni contemporanee usa spesso *while*: *While I was cooking, Marco was setting the table*.
- Negativa: *wasn't/weren't + -ing*. Domanda: *Was/Were + soggetto + -ing?*

### Struttura

| Funzione | Forma | Esempio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Azione in corso | was/were + -ing | She was sleeping at ten. |
| Interruzione | continuous + when + simple | I was driving when you called. |
| Azioni parallele | while + continuous | While I was reading, he was cooking. |

### Esempi con traduzione

- **I was working at six yesterday.** — Ieri alle sei stavo lavorando.
- **They were waiting for the bus.** — Stavano aspettando l'autobus.
- **She wasn't listening.** — Non stava ascoltando.
- **Were you sleeping at midnight?** — Dormivi a mezzanotte?
- **I was having a shower when the phone rang.** — Stavo facendo la doccia quando è squillato il telefono.
- **While we were eating, it started to snow.** — Mentre mangiavamo, ha iniziato a nevicare.
- **Marco fell while he was skiing.** — Marco è caduto mentre sciava.
- **The sun was shining and people were sitting outside.** — Il sole splendeva e le persone erano sedute fuori.

### Errori tipici per chi parla italiano

- ❌ **I watched TV when you called.** → ✅ **I was watching TV when you called.** — Se vuoi sottolineare l'azione già in corso, usa il past continuous.
- ❌ **I was drive when it happened.** → ✅ **I was driving when it happened.** — Dopo *was* serve la forma in *-ing*.
- ❌ **While I cooked, he was reading.** → ✅ **While I was cooking, he was reading.** — Per due azioni contemporanee in corso, il continuous è la scelta normale.
- ❌ **I was knowing the answer.** → ✅ **I knew the answer.** — *Know* è normalmente un verbo di stato.

### Esercizio guidato

1. Completa: I ___ (sleep) at eleven. **Risposta: was sleeping.**
2. Completa: They ___ (play) when it rained. **Risposta: were playing.**
3. Unisci: I walked home. I saw Anna. **Risposta: I was walking home when I saw Anna.**
4. Completa: While she was cooking, I ___ (set) the table. **Risposta: was setting.**

### Esercizio finale

1. Completa: At nine, we ___ (watch) a film.
2. Completa: She ___ (not work) when I called.
3. Completa: ___ you ___ (sleep) at midnight?
4. Scegli: I *walked / was walking* home when I found the wallet.
5. Scegli: The lights went out while we *ate / were eating*.
6. Correggi: *He was play football.*
7. Correggi: *I was understanding the question.*
8. Traduci: «Stavo guidando quando ha iniziato a piovere.»
9. Traduci: «Mentre studiavo, mia sorella guardava la TV.»
10. Scrivi una frase con un'azione lunga interrotta da un evento breve.

### Soluzioni e spiegazioni

1. **were watching**.
2. **wasn't working**.
3. **Were you sleeping?**
4. **was walking** per l'azione in corso; *found* è l'evento breve.
5. **were eating**.
6. **He was playing football.**
7. **I understood the question.**
8. **I was driving when it started to rain.**
9. **While I was studying, my sister was watching TV.**
10. Modello: **I was having dinner when the doorbell rang.** Sono valide altre combinazioni coerenti.

### Riepilogo

- Forma: *was/were + -ing*.
- Continuous = azione in corso o sfondo; simple = evento concluso.
- Usa spesso *when* per l'interruzione e *while* per la durata.
- I verbi di stato normalmente non usano il continuous.
